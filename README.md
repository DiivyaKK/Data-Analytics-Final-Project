AQI Air Quality Data (India, 2015-2024)

1. Project Overview:
   
   A Python data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and visualization project using the AQI Air Quality Data (India, 2015–2024) dataset. Analyses 10 years    of daily air quality measurements across major Indian cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, and Bangalore) to evaluate pollutant distributions, 
   identify peak pollution months and seasonal spikes,and provide data-driven insights for air quality monitoring.

2. Tools & Libraries
   
     • Language: Python 3

     • Environment: Jupyter Notebook

     • Libraries: pandas, NumPy, matplotlib, seaborn

3. Problem Statement & Objectives:
   
    • Rapid urbanization, industrial growth, and seasonal weather changes have made air pollution a major environmental concern across Indian cities.

    • Analysing 10 years of historical air quality data (2015–2024) is essential to understand how pollution levels have evolved, which cities 
      are most affected, and which pollutants pose the greatest risk.

    • Analyse how India's overall AQI has changed from 2015 to 2024

6. Expected Output:
    
      • Data visualizations and summarized findings presenting key AQI patterns and data-driven insights for pollution control across Indian cities.
